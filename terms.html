Project Health Check scope

The Project Health Check is a review of the documents you provide: typically the schedule, budget or cost report, change-order, RFI and submittal logs, recent OAC minutes, and a contract summary. The report includes a one-page red/yellow/green executive summary, a top 10 risk register with owners and actions, a critical-path, float and long-lead review, a contingency burn and change-order pushback list, notice, documentation and closeout gaps, and a 30-day action plan.

The review is based solely on the documents provided and their accuracy. It is an owner-side advisory review. It is not legal advice, engineering advice, an audit, or a guarantee of project outcome, and it does not replace your own project team, counsel, or design professionals.
