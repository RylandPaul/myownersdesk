Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

The Owner's Desk is a service of Trailhead Standard LLC ("we," "us"). This policy describes what information we collect through myownersdesk.com and our email contact, and how we use it. This is a brochure and sales site; we keep data collection to the minimum needed to answer inquiries and deliver what you buy.

What we collect

  • Information you send us by email. When you contact us at ownersdesk@trailheadstandard.com, we receive your name, email address, and whatever you include in your message. For a Project Health Check, that includes the project documents you choose to share (schedules, budgets, logs, minutes, contract summaries).
  • Order information. Purchases are made through Gumroad. Gumroad shares your name, email address, and order details with us so we can deliver your purchase and provide support.
  • Basic site logs. Our hosting provider records standard technical logs (pages visited, browser type, approximate location) for security and operation of the site.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and discuss whether a service fits your project.
  • To perform a Project Health Check you have purchased, using the documents you provide.
  • To deliver digital products and provide order support.
  • To operate, secure, and improve this website.

Project documents you share for a Health Check are treated as confidential and are used only to perform that review. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for third-party marketing.

Payments

All payments on this site are processed by Gumroad and its payment partner Stripe. We never see, receive, or store your full payment card number. Gumroad's and Stripe's own privacy policies govern the payment information they process.

Third-party services

We rely on a small set of providers to run this site and fulfill orders: Gumroad (storefront and checkout), Stripe (payment processing through Gumroad), GitHub Pages (website hosting), and our email provider. Each receives only the information needed for its function.

Retention and your choices

We keep inquiry emails and Health Check working files only as long as needed to complete the work and meet ordinary business record obligations. You may ask us at any time what information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing ownersdesk@trailheadstandard.com.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions about privacy can be sent to ownersdesk@trailheadstandard.com.
